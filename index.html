「格物」二字The two characters

「格物」讲的是穷究事物之理：把一样东西放到眼前，一层层拆开，弄清它的结构、边界和由来。《礼记·大学》里「致知在格物」，说的就是这个次序 —— 先格物，才有真知。

放在今天：每隔几周就有新模型、新范式、新叙事。热度本身不是知识。Materin 想做的，是热点出现时先把它拆开 —— 它到底是什么、能做什么、边界在哪、宣传和事实差多少 —— 然后再讲出来。

格物 is about getting to the bottom of things: put something in front of you, take it apart layer by layer, and work out its structure, its edges and where it came from. The Book of Rites puts it as "knowledge comes from investigating things" — first investigate, then know.

Today: a new model, a new paradigm, a new narrative every few weeks. Attention is not knowledge. What Materin does is take the thing apart first — what it actually is, what it can do, where its limits are, how far the pitch is from the facts — and then explain it.

做法How it's done

  • 先看清，再下判断Look closely before judging
  • 能验证的说，不能验证的不说Say what can be verified; skip the rest
  • 把复杂的东西讲到不需要背景知识Explain it so no background is required
