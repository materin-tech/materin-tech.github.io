Understand it first.
Then explain it plainly.

「格物」二字The two characters

「格物」的意思是把一件事追到底：它从哪来、由什么构成、边界在哪。《礼记·大学》讲「致知在格物」—— 先弄清楚，才有真知。

换成今天的话：技术每隔几周换一批，热闹的多，讲明白的少。这个站点愿意花时间做后者 —— 把东西拆开看清，再用不必懂行也能读懂的话写出来。

格物 means following a thing to the bottom: where it came from, what it's made of, where its edges are. The Book of Rites puts it as "knowledge comes from investigating things" — get it straight first, then you know.

In today's terms: technology turns over every few weeks, and there is far more noise than explanation. This site spends its time on the latter — open the thing up, then write it down in words that need no background.

做法How it's done

  • 先弄清，再评论Understand first, comment after
  • 只写验证过的Only what can be checked
  • 外行也能读懂Readable without background
